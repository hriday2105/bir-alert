Bir Alert

Early warning for desert wells. It predicts how many days a well has left, says how sure it is, texts the village in plain language, and sends each village to a different backup well so the warning does not cause a stampede.

What the village receives

Data honesty: this demo uses simulated wells: a water balance driven by a synthetic drought-year rainfall and heat series for a Marsabit-style region. Well names are only labels. The same pipeline takes CHIRPS rainfall, ERA5-Land evaporation and Sentinel-2 water and vegetation indices from Google Earth Engine. Sensor loss: falls back to the regional trend from neighbouring wells and widens the range. Cloud: widens it again and switches to Landsat or radar. Forecasts use a rolling trend fit with 80% ranges, and alert thresholds come from the cost slider.
